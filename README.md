# Thicker Than Mud

A kavatender's guide to organizing your bar.

Solidarity is thicker than mud.

## What this is

A printable zine for kava workers. It covers your rights, the steps from shell to contract, the boss's playbook, and who's got your back. Issue 02 digs deeper into the working conditions Issue 01 touched on: wage theft, scheduling, closing alone, bodies, surveillance and discipline, where the money goes, and the general union.

The zine is free. Copy it, fold it, pass it on.

## Which size should I print?

Print the **narrow quarter** version first. It is 3.5 by 5.5 inches, it fits a normal stapler, and it reads well. Each issue has a reading PDF for reading on screen, and a print PDF for folding into a booklet.

Print the **eighth** version if you want a pocket copy to hand out at a bar. It is 4.25 by 2.75 inches, 24 pages for Issue 01. Note: it is still 4.25 inches wide, so it needs a long reach stapler, a copy shop, or stapling by hand. It will not fit a normal desktop stapler.

Other sizes were made along the way (full quarter, sixth, narrow sixth). They are not in this repo, because narrow quarter does their job better, or because they do not fit a normal stapler. They are kept in a local archive only.

## How to print the narrow quarter version

For Issue 01 (12 pages, 3 folios) and Issue 02 (16 pages, 4 folios). The method is the same, only the number of folios changes.

1. Print the print PDF double sided, flip on long edge, at 100 percent.
2. Cut on the single vertical dashed orange line, 7 inches from the left edge. Set the 1.5 inch strip on the right aside.
3. Cut once per sheet on the horizontal dashed line across the middle, 5.5 inches from the top. That gives two 7 by 5.5 inch strips per sheet.
4. Fold each strip in half vertically, fold on the left. Check the front is an odd page. If you see an even page instead, unfold and fold it the other way.
5. Nest the folios in order and staple twice along the spine.
   - Issue 01: 1 and 2 with 11 and 12 outside, then 3 and 4 with 9 and 10, then 5 and 6 with 7 and 8 inside. The bottom strip on the second sheet is blank, set it aside.
   - Issue 02: 1 and 2 with 15 and 16 outside, then 3 and 4 with 13 and 14, then 5 and 6 with 11 and 12, then 7 and 8 with 9 and 10 inside.

## How to print the eighth version (Issue 01)

1. Print the print PDF double sided, flip on long edge, at 100 percent.
2. Cut each sheet into 4 strips on the dashed orange lines, 3 cuts per sheet.
3. Fold each strip in half vertically, fold on the left. Check the front is an odd page. If you see an even page instead, unfold and fold it the other way.
4. Nest the 6 folios in order and staple twice along the spine. Discard the blank strips from the second sheet.
