# Contributing

Thank you for helping. A few rules keep this repo safe and useful.

## What to contribute

- Corrections to facts, phone numbers, links, or law in the zine.
- Translations, or adaptations for your city or state. Say what you changed and why.
- New issues, if you have talked with the maintainers first.

## What not to add

- Personal information about workers or bars without their explicit say so. No names, no phone numbers, no addresses, no screenshots with group chat names.
- Anything that identifies a worker who is organizing. If in doubt, leave it out and ask a maintainer.
- Recipes, employer documents, or anything you do not have the right to share. If you are unsure about the rights to something, ask before you open a pull request.

## How to add a new issue

1. Put the reading PDF and the print PDF in `issues/issue-XX/`, in a folder named for the size, like `narrow-quarter/`.
2. Name the files clearly: `thicker-than-mud-issue-XX-narrow-quarter-reading.pdf` and `thicker-than-mud-issue-XX-narrow-quarter-print-folded.pdf`.
3. Update the issues table in README.md, and add the print instructions if the method is different.
4. If the issue is a new size, say so in the pull request, and say why that size is needed. We keep the number of sizes small on purpose, so people download the right file.

## Safety

Do not commit from a work computer or on bar Wi-Fi. Use your own phone or computer, and your own network. If you are organizing at your bar, talk to an organizer at EWOC, workerorganizing.org, before you put anything in this repo that could identify you or your coworkers.
